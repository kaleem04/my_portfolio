FAQ

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the site and which follow-up features you need. I'll give you a clear quote after a quick call. There are no surprise fees.

How long does it take?

A typical small-business site goes live in about a week after I have your content and photos.

What do I need to provide?

Your services, service area, logo, a few photos, and where you want requests sent (email or phone).

Do you use AI?

Yes, for the website assistant and drafting. It answers common questions from your own business information and hands off anything it shouldn't handle to you.

Where are you based and when can we talk?

I'm in Pakistan and work with U.S. clients, available by appointment during U.S. Eastern and Central daytime hours.

Do you make cold calls or auto-texts?

No. Everything I set up responds only to customers who contact you first.
